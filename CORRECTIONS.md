# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to hartree-small-charge-rigidity 1.0.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23269957)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v1.0.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/hartree-small-charge-rigidity/>.

1. Corrected a statement of domain in §3, in the paragraph before the definition of F. The archived text says g is C¹ on U × (−q*, q*). Lemma 2.2 proves only that g has a C¹ extension to an open neighbourhood of (0, 0). The sentence should read: “Section 2 proves that g is C¹ on an open neighbourhood of (0, 0) in C^{2,β}(S²) × ℝ, with values in C^{1,β}(S²).”

   The proof uses g only near (0, 0), so it is unaffected. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
